## Situation

An automotive recycling company, a subsidiary of a large French automotive group,
with a network of partner dealerships. Three processes ran on Excel, e-mail and PDFs, each with the same
symptoms: non-uniform requests, manual follow-up, no shared view of status, delays and
errors. The IT department of a group that size does not staff tools for a subsidiary's
operations team. Each time, the operations side bought the tool directly.

## Task

Three bounded applications, each replacing one manual process, each handed to the
team that runs it. Delivered as the builder, through the agency that introduced the
client, on Bubble, with Make and Mailjet where automation was needed.

## The three applications

**1. A parts marketplace across dealerships.** Second-hand auto parts from partner
dealerships, listed for professionals. Inventory refreshed nightly from an SFTP feed
into Bubble via Make; product catalogue with specifications; professional accounts
with a cart; automated HTML e-mail summaries to administrators for each request.
Designed, built and tested in two weeks.

**2. A multi-actor recycling workflow for electric-vehicle batteries.** Dealerships
submitted requests through dynamic forms; the app generated the PDFs, sent the e-mails
through Mailjet, and gave the internal team a Kanban dashboard that refreshed on its
own. It replaced Excel sheets, manual e-mails and hand-made PDFs.

**3. A dealership onboarding system.** A central request form with pre-validation in
the backend, role-based administration for assistants and advisors, automated e-mail
sequences, and a custom one-time-code login for secure access without accounts.
Administrators handled more requests with fewer delays; dealerships got one
transparent process.

## Results

- Three applications delivered to the same client, one a year in 2023, 2024 and 2025,
  the third one bought on the strength of the first two.
- Application 1 delivered in two weeks from design to tested product.
- All three still in use in September 2026.

## What you get

A manual process replaced by an application with a bounded scope, handed to the
team that runs it. Your IT department does not have to staff it. This client
bought the second and the third application on the strength of the first.
