## Situation | Customers needed to book from their phone.

[Clean Car France](https://cleancar-france.fr/) prepares and cleans vehicles for private and business customers. It wanted its customers to book a service from their phone, and its team to see every job in one calendar.

## Task | Build the booking app, through the agency NoxCod.

Build the app in Bubble for the agency NoxCod, from the data model to the admin side, so it could ship to the App Store and Google Play.

## Actions | A mobile booking app, built in 17 days.

- I designed the data model and built the sign-up and login, with a private and a business profile.
- I built the request form: several vehicles in one request, a service type for each (new, used, utility or demo vehicle), and extra services such as tyre shine or headlight renovation.
- I built the customer's list of requests and the profile pages.
- I built the admin side and its calendar, by day, week or month, filtered by team member.
- The app is on the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/lv/app/clean-car/id6463682542) and [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.cleancarapp.cleancarapp&pli=1).

![Admin calendar](/assets/projects/clean-car/f1a26tsm1zbvfgljsppe.webp)

## Results | Built in 17 days, now on both stores.

- The first screens on 8 May 2023, the last part (the admin side) on 24 May: 17 days.
- The final web version on 21 June 2023, after the client's feedback.
- Now on the App Store and Google Play.
