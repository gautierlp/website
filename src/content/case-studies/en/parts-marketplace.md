## Situation | The client wanted its network to find parts online.

The client, an automotive recycling company, collects second-hand parts from its partner dealerships. It wanted the professionals in its network to find and request those parts online, from a catalogue that stays current without manual work.

## Task | A parts catalogue that stays current on its own.

Let the professionals in the network find and request the parts online, from a catalogue that stays current without manual work.

## Actions | A marketplace fed each night, built in two weeks.

- A nightly import. Make reads the dealerships' SFTP feed and updates the Bubble database with the photos, descriptions, prices and locations of the parts.
- A catalogue that shows the year, brand, vehicle type and dealership of each part.
- Professional accounts, approved by an admin, with a cart.
- An HTML summary email to the admins for each validated cart.

## Results | Professionals request parts from a catalogue updated each night.

- Designed, built and tested in two weeks.
- One of three applications built for this client, one a year from 2023 to 2025. All three were still in use in September 2026.
