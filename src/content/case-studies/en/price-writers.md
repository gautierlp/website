## Situation | The contracts had no app.

Price Writers is an insurance business in London. It needed an app to keep its insurance contracts in one place and to see their numbers.

## Task | Build the whole app from basic wireframes.

Build the app, front end and back end, from basic wireframes, with a free hand on the final design.

## Actions | One page, every contract, the numbers on top.

- **One page.** A single-page app, built in Bubble, so the screens change without a reload.
- **The contracts.** Create, read, change and delete each insurance contract.
- **The numbers.** Statistics on the contracts, on the same page.

## Results | Delivered the same day.

- The whole app built in one day, on 19 January 2023.
- The client wrote: "Gautier did an excellent job. The project was delivered quickly and meets our expectations perfectly. We are very satisfied and highly recommend his services."
