A subsidiary of a large French automotive group that runs a network of vehicle recycling centres. I built three applications for it: an app to collect used electric-vehicle batteries from dealerships, a site where car repairers order used parts held by the client's recycling centres, and a portal where recycling centres apply to join the network.

All three started in 2023: the battery app in August, the parts site in October, the portal in November. The parts site went live in December 2023, the portal in July 2024. I kept changing them until January 2026.
