## Introduction

Disko Leads, founded by Johary, is a startup specializing in lead generation through LinkedIn data extraction and enrichment, helping businesses gain actionable insights from LinkedIn interactions. Disko Leads sought a platform to allow users to generate high-value leads by pulling LinkedIn profile data from individuals who liked or commented on specific posts.

![image](/assets/projects/disko-leads/vun6l99nj5abgqbgl7f7.webp)

---

## Problem statement

### Core Challenges

Johary needed a reliable LinkedIn data extraction tool that would simplify lead generation for users. The app had to support user-friendly filtering of LinkedIn interactions, profile enrichment with external data, and organized exports to CSV. Additionally, it required a seamless credit and payment system, along with an admin interface for efficient user and data management.

![image](/assets/projects/disko-leads/b3iffjhh1ic6lg0f8iqk.webp)

### Key Business Risks

Without a robust, feature-rich tool, Disko Leads risked a weak market entry, which could hinder their ability to attract early adopters and secure revenue. A fast-deployment, high-quality solution was essential to deliver an attractive, user-friendly experience that would foster adoption and retention.

---

## Solution

### Approach

I worked closely with Johary to design and develop their application from the ground up. My approach focused on creating a seamless user experience for LinkedIn data extraction, combining a Chrome extension and a web app. Within three weeks, I developed a fully functional platform using [Bubble.io](http://Bubble.io) for the web application and JavaScript for the Chrome extension.

![image](/assets/projects/disko-leads/nx41sapeewr8tldkfuia.webp)

### Core Solution Components

- **Chrome Extension:** Built a Chrome extension with an extraction button on each LinkedIn post, which redirected users to Disko Leads’ web app for easy access.

- **Web App Features:**

- **Data Extraction and Enrichment:** Users could filter based on engagement type (e.g., likes/comments), industry, job title, and company size, generating CSV files with LinkedIn profile data. The app further enriched profiles through [RapidAPI](https://rapidapi.com/) for additional insights.

- **Credit and Payment System:** The app deducted credits for each CSV generation, integrated with [Stripe](https://stripe.com/) for smooth payment and credit purchases.

- **Admin Dashboard:** Enabled Johary to manage users and oversee CSV exports.

- **Integrations:** The app included [SendGrid](https://sendgrid.com/) for automated email communication and Google Sheets for data visualization, enhancing overall user experience.

---

## Implementation

### Development and Timeline

In a three-week period, I designed, built, and tested the platform to ensure a smooth experience across devices. I prioritized an intuitive interface for CSV generation, credit management, and filtering options.

![image](/assets/projects/disko-leads/tnsih75zniqiccfypzmq.webp)

### Technology Stack

The web app was built with [Bubble.io](http://Bubble.io) for rapid development, offering real-time user management and integrated payments. The Chrome extension, built with JavaScript, handled LinkedIn data extraction. APIs streamlined profile enrichment via [RapidAPI](https://rapidapi.com/), and additional integrations included [Stripe](https://stripe.com/), [SendGrid](https://sendgrid.com/), and [Google Sheets](https://workspace.google.com/intl/fr/products/sheets/) for payment processing, email automation, and data visualization.

### User Adoption and Revenue

Disko Leads launched successfully, quickly gaining traction and reaching $3,000 MRR soon after launch. The platform enabled users to extract valuable lead data efficiently, supporting strong initial retention and steady recurring revenue.

![image](/assets/projects/disko-leads/gum2cyaz9psc7x0zpxcv.webp)

### User Satisfaction

With its intuitive design and integrated automation, the app provided a user-friendly experience, allowing users to easily manage and purchase extractions through the automated credit system.
