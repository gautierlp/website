# Launching a Bubble app on App Store and Google Play

## Introduction

[Clean Car France](https://cleancar-france.fr/) specializes in vehicle care services across several categories, catering to both personal and business clients. Their [offerings](https://cleancar-france.fr/nos-services/) include services for new and used car preparation, showroom demo setups, and vehicle deliveries. To streamline the customer booking experience and improve service management, Clean Car France identified the need for a mobile app that could manage bookings and schedule workforce tasks effectively.

![image](/assets/projects/clean-car/w7gkrwo3toydrww07npn.webp)

To support their growth, Clean Car France required a mobile solution allowing customers to easily book various services for different vehicle types. Simultaneously, the app needed to allow administrators to assign employees to tasks in a streamlined, efficient way. This digital shift aimed to scale operations, enhance scheduling, and simplify the customer booking process.

---

## Problem statement

### Core Challenges 

The key challenge involved developing a responsive app that could manage a range of customer bookings for various service types and vehicle categories. Additionally, Clean Car France required a calendar-based interface to efficiently assign tasks to employees. The app needed to cater to both individual and business clients, allowing the latter to book services for multiple vehicles simultaneously.

![image](/assets/projects/clean-car/gpflqpc9ql4ixsxfpma4.webp)

### Key Business Risks

Without a robust and accessible booking app, Clean Car France faced potential operational inefficiencies and risks to customer satisfaction. A streamlined process for handling bookings and assignments was essential to meet rising demand and provide a seamless experience for clients and employees alike.

---

## Solution

### Approach

I created a fully responsive mobile app on [Bubble.io](http://Bubble.io) within a 10-day timeframe, specifically designed to meet the needs of Clean Car France’s customers and administrative team. The app was optimized for mobile use and published on both the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/lv/app/clean-car/id6463682542) and [Google Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.cleancarapp.cleancarapp&pli=1) to maximize accessibility.

![image](/assets/projects/clean-car/jc8vv6m3lwlpjdktp3jf.webp)

### Core Solution Components

- **Customer Features**: Users can sign up, log in, and book services tailored to different vehicle types, such as new, used, demonstration, and utility vehicle preparations, as well as vehicle transport. The app accommodates bookings for single or multiple services across one or more vehicles, which suits both individual and professional clients.

- **Admin Features**: Customer orders are directed to an admin dashboard where Clean Car France’s team can assign tasks to employees using a calendar-based view. The intuitive interface makes it easy for admins to allocate tasks visually, track progress, and manage resources effectively.

---

## Implementation

### Development and Timeline

Within 10 days, the app was designed, built, and launched using [Bubble.io](http://Bubble.io), ensuring full responsiveness and accessibility across devices. Rigorous testing was conducted to ensure the app met Clean Car France’s functional, reliable, and user-friendly requirements.

![image](/assets/projects/clean-car/f1a26tsm1zbvfgljsppe.webp)

### Technology Stack

Leveraging [Bubble.io](http://Bubble.io)’s no-code capabilities, I rapidly developed the app and published it on both the App Store and Google Play Store, ensuring broad platform availability for customers.

---

## Results

### Operational Efficiency

The app significantly improved the booking and task assignment processes. With the calendar view for assignments, admins could organize tasks more effectively, resulting in higher productivity and streamlined task management.

![image](/assets/projects/clean-car/egqourhn1nylwlviqsqh.webp)

### Customer Experience

The app provided a quick, straightforward booking experience for various car care services, enhancing accessibility for both individual and professional clients. Its presence on major app stores increased Clean Car France’s reach and visibility.
